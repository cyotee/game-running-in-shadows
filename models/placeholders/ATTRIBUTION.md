## Notes

- Some Poly Pizza pages list older models under **CC-BY 3.0**; this review set intentionally used models listed as **CC0 1.0**.
- Do not treat these as final IP-safe *branding* — only as free geometry. Avoid any resemblance to third-party trademarks in marketing assets.
- Spell *effects* are not mesh packs; use Godot `GPUParticles3D` + free particle textures (e.g. Brackeys VFX Bundle, CC0) later.
