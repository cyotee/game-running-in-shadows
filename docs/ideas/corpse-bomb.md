# Corpse Bomb

**Type:** Cybernetic (Augment) — **Full Body slot** (mutually exclusive with Smart Rig and Overclock Rig)
**Status:** Idea — recorded 2026-07-16

## Concept (owner spec)

- The owner's corpse **explodes when: looted, destroyed, or an enemy player moves within 2 meters.**
- Large AoE.
- Intent: make players very cautious about approaching enemy bodies.
- **Noted high-risk strategy:** buy the bomb early, rush the enemy team, die among them, and detonate for a multi-kill and a large early-round kill payment.

## Interactions with decided systems

- **Slot cost is the balance:** Full Body means giving up Smart Rig AND Overclock for a death-triggered effect — you pay with your whole living kit. Elegant.
- **Corpse economy pillar:** directly weaponizes the looting/denial decisions — looting becomes a minesweeping decision; Devour (idea) triggering the bomb ("destroyed" trigger) makes devourers gamble too. Reclaim near a bombed *enemy* corpse is fine, but the 2 m proximity trigger makes mixed-body brawls spicy.
- **Friendly fire:** full FF is decided — does the blast hurt the owner's teammates defending the corpse? Presumably yes (consistent), which self-limits corpse-camping your own bomb.
- **Explosive damage type:** unblockable by blade; Armor Skin (idea) reduces it.
- **Economy check on the rush strategy:** kill payments to a dead player still accrue (decided economy pays performance bonuses) — the strategy works as intended. Watch for degenerate suicide-rush metas; the counter is shooting the corpse from range (triggering "destroyed" safely).

## Open questions

- Does the explosion destroy the corpse itself (self-denying Reclaim on the owner — probably yes, and part of the cost)?
- Ally proximity: enemy-only trigger at 2 m confirmed — corpse-dragging abilities later could reposition the trap.
- Visual tell: none (pure trap), subtle (chrome glint for observant players), or Pulse Sight reveals it? Recommend *some* counterable tell.
- Damage/radius baseline vs grenade (larger AoE per spec — how much?).
- One corpse only (most recent) or every corpse within the per-player cap?
