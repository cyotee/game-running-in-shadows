# Null Core

**Type:** Cybernetic (Augment) — **Organ slot** (mutually exclusive with Second Heart)
**Status:** Idea (owner-approved direction) — recorded 2026-07-17

## Concept (owner spec)

- Self-only resistance to enemy Manifestation effects — a pocket null-field. **"Poor man's 'Be a Dwarf'"** (owner's words).
- Examples: weakened Tangle pull, dampened Death Field ticks, reduced Wraith-burn from enemy Repulse, faster recovery from Manifestation debuffs generally.

## Interactions with decided systems

- **Organ pairing:** sustain (Second Heart) vs anti-magic (Null Core). NOTE: with Adrenal Surge rejected (2026-07-17, Overclock overlap), the Organ slot sits at TWO options — one short of the trilemma benchmark. Third candidate wanted (must not overlap Overclock's tempo or Second Heart's sustain).
- **Dwarf identity guard:** must stay strictly weaker than the Dwarf passive (no drain, no effect-destruction, self-only, percentage resistance not immunity) or it erodes the lineage's reason to exist. Suggested frame: Dwarf *denies* magic around them; Null Core just *endures* it.
- **Tech multiplier irony (good):** a Dwarf buying Null Core pays ×2.0 for redundancy — self-balancing. The natural customers are Humans/Trolls building anti-caster without playing Dwarf.
- **Interaction sweep when promoting:** exact resistance vs each Manifestation (Tangle pull %, Death Field tick %, Repulse force %, Slip/Wraith unaffected?, does it dampen ALLIED Lifebloom healing on you? — suggest no: resist hostile effects only, but "hostile" needs a rule under friend-or-foe abilities).
