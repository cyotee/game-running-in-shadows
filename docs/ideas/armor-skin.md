# Armor Skin

**Type:** Cybernetic (Augment) — **Skin slot** (new slot; mutually exclusive with Optical Camouflage)
**Status:** Idea — recorded 2026-07-16

## Concept (owner spec)

- Damage reduction against **all physical damage, including explosive**.
- **Tuned to cancel ALL damage from Pistols** (implies a flat reduction ≥ pistol per-shot damage, i.e. ~12 at current baselines, rather than a percentage).

## Interactions with decided systems

- **Damage model:** a flat-per-hit reduction naturally makes low-damage-per-hit weapons (Pistol 12, SMG 7 → fully negated too at flat 12!) useless while barely denting Sniper (55). **Flag: at current baselines a flat 12 cancels the SMG as well as the Pistol** — either intended (anti-light-weapons skin) or the tune should sit between SMG and Pistol per-hit. Owner said "cancel Pistols"; SMG cancellation may be a surprise.
- **Shotgun interaction:** per-pellet damage 6 vs flat reduction = shotguns fully negated at any range. Almost certainly needs per-pellet vs per-shot reduction rules.
- **Type bypasses:** spirit damage (Resonance Bolt, Death Field — one type) ignores it by design. Melee? Bleed (it's a DoT, not physical per-hit)? Define.
- **Troll + Armor Skin:** stacks with Harden — the resulting fortress is the tank-meta test case.
- **Slot rules:** Skin slot, permanent, excludes Optical Camouflage (tank vs stealth commitment).

## Open questions
