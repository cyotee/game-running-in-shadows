# Capacitor Skin

**Type:** Cybernetic (Augment) — **Skin slot** (mutually exclusive with Optical Camouflage, Armor Skin)
**Status:** Idea (owner-approved direction) — recorded 2026-07-17

## Concept (owner spec)

- Converts a fraction of damage taken into Resonance — profit-from-pain, the caster's skin.
- **Consideration: charge may fill OVER locked pips** — damage-derived Resonance can exceed the normal cap (max − reserved), temporarily granting access to pool space locked by reservations.
- Intended fantasy: **take damage to go big and loud** — fire off a burst of abilities mid-fight. The user will probably die, but the surge can drive a push, or kill enough of the enemy team to change the tide of a round.

## Interactions with decided systems

- **Completes the Skin trilemma:** dodge damage (Camo) / shrug damage (Armor) / eat damage for power (Capacitor).
- **Over-cap charging is a genuinely new pool mechanic:** the decided model is `cap = max − reserved`, hard ceiling. Overcharge needs rules: does over-cap Resonance decay when the fighting stops? Does it unlock casting only, or also feed reservations? Suggest: overcharge decays (~1/s) once out of combat, spend-first ordering (overcharge burns before normal pool).
- **Any-combat synergy is the engine:** taking damage freezes regen — Capacitor Skin is the *only* way to gain Resonance while being shot. That's the whole identity, and it's cleanly emergent.
- **Loop check (important):** self-damage sources (Overclock Overdrive drain, Corpse Bomb, own grenades under full FF) must NOT charge the skin, or degenerate self-charge loops appear. Rule: only enemy-inflicted damage charges. Death Field ticks (enemy's) would charge it — a Capacitor user *wants* to stand in a Death Field; delightful or degenerate, playtest.
- **Elf interplay:** Elf auto-heal spends Resonance for health; Capacitor converts health-loss into Resonance — an Elf Capacitor build approaches a perpetual-motion feel at equilibrium. Check the exchange rates don't close the loop (heal 1 Res → 8 HP; charge should be well below 8 HP → 1 Res).

## Open questions

- Conversion rate baseline (12? 16? HP per 1 Resonance) and whether headshot damage charges more.
- Overcharge ceiling (max +2 pips? up to full max ignoring reservations?), decay rate, and HUD (glowing pips over the locked ones?).
- Reservation cost of the skin itself (which the overcharge can then... fill past? — the recursion is the point).
