# Hopper Legs

**Type:** Cybernetic (Augment) — **Leg slot** (new slot; mutually exclusive with Cheetah Legs)
**Status:** Idea — recorded 2026-07-16

## Concept (owner spec)

- Doubles jump height and jump distance.
- No cooldown — **players can be the pogo stick.**

## Interactions with decided systems

- **Movement tech stack:** interacts multiplicatively with Repulse launches and Glide — a Hopper+Glide player has a serious verticality kit without spending Resonance per jump. Check map ceilings and the combo tuning.
- **Contextual A-button:** airborne jump deploys glide (controller decision) — a double-height jump then A again = glide deploy; feels natural, no conflict.
- **Slot rules:** Leg slot, permanent, excludes Cheetah Legs (vertical vs horizontal mobility commitment).
- **Movement weight:** does carried-weapon weight scale the jump bonus (heavy sniper Hopper = less bounce)? Suggest yes for consistency.
- **Fall damage:** none currently exists in the prototype; pogo play may force the fall-damage question.

## Open questions

- Reservation baseline (1? 2? — mobility chrome priced like Glide Harness?).
- Does "distance" doubling apply to air control or just launch velocity?
- Anti-cheat validation: movement caps in the Layer-1 validation must account for legit Hopper velocities.
