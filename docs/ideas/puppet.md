# Puppet

**Type:** Manifestation
**Status:** Idea (owner-approved direction) — recorded 2026-07-17

## Concept (owner spec)

- Animate a corpse as a minion.
- The corpse is **locked while puppeted** — no Reclaim, no looting — and **destroyed if the puppet dies**.
- **Must be more powerful than Familiar** to justify the corpse requirement. Candidate power directions (owner's brainstorm — pick/combine when promoting):
  - A smaller Death Field aura on the puppet.
  - Some amount of innate damage reduction.
  - Wraith-like phasing while ≥3 meters from all enemies (materializes to strike).
- **Feel target (owner's words):** a scary zombie — deadly if it gets close, or very hard to kill. A relentless killer that could drive its target off.

## Interactions with decided systems

- **Completes two trilemmas at once:** summons — creature (Familiar) / illusion (Clone) / corpse (Puppet); corpse spells — revive (Reclaim) / consume (Devour) / weaponize (Puppet).
- **Corpse economy stakes:** puppeting an ENEMY corpse denies their Reclaim *and* threatens them with their own teammate's body — maximum drama. Puppeting an ALLIED corpse spends your own team's revive resource for a weapon — real cost. Both-directions choice is the design's heart.
- **The phase-approach option is the standout** for the "relentless" feel: a puppet that's untargetable at range and materializes at 3 m inverts normal engagement rules — you cannot poke it down, you must reposition or burst it point-blank. Terrifying and unique vs Familiar (which fights normally). If combined with modest DR, cap its damage so the terror is the chase, not instant death.
- **Death Field aura option** cross-references the Death Field carve-outs (always ticks, hurts everyone — a puppet aura should probably not hurt the puppeteer's team, or it does and that's the price).
- **Counters (must hold):** Null Charge destroys it (Manifestation), Dwarf aura drains it, killing the puppeteer kills it (effects die with caster), destroying it destroys the corpse (Devour-adjacent denial — enemies may WANT to kill the puppet to deny Reclaim... wait, the corpse was already locked; killing the puppet completes the denial FOR the enemy. Interesting: puppeting your own dead teammate then losing the puppet = you denied yourself. Stakes work.)
- **Reservation:** held while active (4-5, above Familiar's 4) per the more-powerful-than-Familiar rule; empty-return on death applies.
- **AI scope:** shares the Familiar nav/AI work; ping-targeting presumably also steers it (ping system prerequisite).

## Open questions

- Does the puppet's appearance reveal whose corpse it was (readable identity = teams know their Reclaim is being held hostage)? Suggest yes.
