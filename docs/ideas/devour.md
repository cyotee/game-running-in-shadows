# Devour

**Type:** Manifestation
**Status:** Idea — recorded 2026-07-16

## Concept (owner spec)

- **Destroys a corpse to restore the caster's health.**
- An unlooted corpse **drops its gear** when devoured (the gear isn't consumed — it spills).
- No effect on living players.

## Interactions with decided systems

- **Corpse economy pillar:** this is triple-purpose — self-heal + body denial (destroys the corpse → no Reclaim) + loot piñata. It makes corpses contested by BOTH teams for different reasons (enemy: devour/deny; allies: defend for Reclaim). Excellent fit with the decided corpse defense/destruction/looting pillar.
- **Reclaim counter:** devouring is the Manifestation-side body denial (weapons-side is rocket/objective-item destruction per the source modes).
- **Corpse Bomb interaction (idea):** devouring a bombed corpse triggers the bomb? ("destroyed" is a listed trigger) — a devourer's gamble. Note in both files.
- **Corpse limits:** the decided per-player corpse cap interacts — devoured corpses free cap space.

## Open questions
