# Optical Camouflage

**Type:** Cybernetic (Augment) — **Skin slot** (new slot; mutually exclusive with Armor Skin)
**Status:** Idea — recorded 2026-07-16

## Concept (owner spec)

- Adds a transparency effect to the player.
- **Pushes enemy view away from the user**, making aiming at them difficult (inverse aim-assist).
- **Smart Rig users experience an exaggerated view-push and see greater transparency** — the camo specifically defeats the "legal aimbot."

## Interactions with decided systems

- **Counter web:** this is the designed anti-Smart-Rig tool on the *target's* side (complementing Katana+Overdrive on the attacker's side). Smart Rig's forced-view tracking gets actively repelled.
- **Implementation family:** Smart Rig pulls enemy view toward center mass; this pushes view away. Both are signed view-forces — build one system, two signs.
- **Slot rules:** Skin slot, permanent once purchased; excludes Armor Skin (stealth vs tank skin choice).
- **Controller slowdown-on-target:** presumably nullified or inverted against camo users — needs a rule.

## Open questions

- Is the transparency/view-push always on (passive) or an activated mode with Resonance reservation while active?
- Does the view-push affect mouse users' aim (view force on mouse is contentious) or only reduce slowdown-assist/apply to Smart Rig? Define per input path.
- Visibility model: fixed transparency, or speed-based (more visible when moving) like classic cloaks?
- Pulse Sight interaction: does the reveal outline pierce the camo?
- Wraith overlap: both are "hard to see" tools — differentiate (camo = physical, shootable, can act; Wraith = immune, can't act).
