# Power Arm

**Type:** Cybernetic (Augment) — **Arm slot** (mutually exclusive with Spider Rope, if the rope lands as an Arm cybernetic)
**Status:** Idea (owner-approved direction; Stabilizer Arm merged in) — recorded 2026-07-17

## Concept (owner spec)

Single arm cybernetic combining handling and strength (the separate "Stabilizer Arm" proposal is rolled into this):

- **Reduces recoil.**
- **Negates the stagger from Repulse.**
- **Corpse dragging upgrade:** players are normally limited to Pistol or SMG while dragging a corpse (baseline rule — recorded in MECHANICS_REFERENCE §11); Power Arm allows **Rifle or Shotgun** while dragging.
- **Powerful but slow melee: one-hit kill if the enemy has no damage reduction.**
- **Throws Grenades and Null Charges very far.**

## Interactions with decided systems

- **Arm slot count:** with the merge, Arm sits at TWO options (Spider Rope / Power Arm) — one short of the trilemma benchmark. Third candidate wanted (mobility / strength / ??? — something neither grapple nor brawn).
- **OHK melee vs the slow-TTK design:** a 100+ damage strike in a 1.5 s+ TTK game is the hardest-hitting single action in the game. "Slow" must be REALLY slow (long windup, heavy audio tell, big recovery) — think pinned-execution, not spammable punch. Interactions: Troll Harden survives it (has DR) ✓; Armor Skin survives ✓; blade block? (melee type — blockable by Katana? Or is a powered fist "windup" type = unblockable? Type assignment matters a lot here.)
- **Repulse-stagger negation vs Rooted Legs:** Power Arm negates *stagger* while mobile; Rooted Legs negate *displacement* while planted. Requires stagger (aim-flinch) and displacement (velocity push) to be defined as separate components of Repulse when either idea is promoted.
- **Recoil reduction + Smart Rig:** Arm + Eye/Full Body don't conflict — Power Arm + Smart Rig is a legal "cheating gunhand" build; recoil reduction stacking on forced tracking needs a look.
- **Throw range boost** serves the Corpse Bomb rush (throw the trap in), Null Charge zoning from safety, and cooked-grenade snipes. Also: throws deployable cameras far (idea synergy).
- **Corpse economy:** dragging-while-armed is the core value — a Power Arm anchor can reposition bodies under fire with a real weapon out. Pairs with Reclaim (drag bodies to a safe revive spot) and against it (drag enemy bodies away from their team).
