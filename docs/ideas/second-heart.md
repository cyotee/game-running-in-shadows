# Second Heart

**Type:** Cybernetic (Augment) — slot TBD (Organ/Torso slot candidate — would establish another slot family)
**Status:** Idea — recorded 2026-07-16

## Concept (owner spec)

- Continual heal effect similar to the Elf racial, but with **no Resonance drain** — only the standard cybernetic reservation (× archetype tech multiplier).
- **Strong enough to counteract the bleed effect but not overcome it** (heal rate == bleed rate → net zero while bleeding).
- **Explicitly intended:** an Elf with a Second Heart heals *through* bleed (Elf heal + Second Heart vs bleed = net positive) while their Resonance drains to fuel the racial heal.

## Interactions with decided systems

- **Bleed equilibrium chain:** bleed 4 HP/s == Elf heal 4 HP/s == Second Heart 4 HP/s (baseline). Non-Elf + Second Heart bleeding = flat health, no Resonance drain. Elf + Second Heart bleeding = +4 HP/s net, Resonance draining. All emergent from stacking the effects — no special cases, matching the decided bleed philosophy.
- **Any-combat gating (owner-confirmed 2026-07-16):** Second Heart heals **continually, including in combat**. Second Heart and Lifebloom are the only in-combat heals in the design so far — a deliberate, scarce category. (Elf racial remains out-of-combat only.)
- **Tech multiplier irony:** an Elf pays ×1.5 reservation for the heart — the archetype that synergizes most pays most. Good tension, worth keeping.
- **Slot rules:** permanent once purchased.

## Open questions

- Reservation baseline (2? 3?) and heal cap (to max health, presumably).
- Death Field interaction: since both always tick, Second Heart (4 HP/s) vs Death Field (4 HP/s) = net-zero HP while the field is up — and a Second Heart **Elf** out-heals it entirely (+4 net) while paying Resonance. Confirm this stack is intended when either idea is promoted.
