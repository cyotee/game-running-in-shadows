# Shield

**Type:** Manifestation
**Status:** Idea — recorded 2026-07-16

## Concept (owner spec)

- Forward-facing damage reduction.
- Floats in front of the player.
- **Size scales with the caster's archetype** (Troll shield large, Elf shield small).

## Interactions with decided systems

- **Held effect:** natural fit for a Resonance reservation while active; empty-return on drop applies; dies with caster; Null/Dwarf drain destroys it — all decided mechanics slot in cleanly.
- **Archetype scaling:** first ability whose *geometry* scales by archetype — pairs thematically with blade damage scaling (melee_mult). A big Troll with a big shield anchoring a push is strong identity.
- **Damage types:** blocks physical/direct presumably; spirit/soul bypass by type rule; explosive splash around it? Pellet spread partially blocked (geometry does the work naturally)?
- **Movement:** does it slow the caster while held (classic shield-wall trade) or is Resonance reservation the whole price?
- **Repulse interaction:** can Repulse shove the shield aside / knock the carrier back through their own shield? Good counterplay candidate.

## Open questions

- Reduction model: full block (a barrier with HP) vs percentage reduction through it?
- Can the caster shoot through their own shield (one-way) — and can allies?
- Reservation baseline (2-3 held) and whether it also blocks the caster's view edges (cost of safety).
